UCCU Stadium Grand Opening

Design Lead  ·  Identity, motion & signage

Overview

The UCCU Stadium was a $28.5M donor gift to UVU. As design lead, I shaped the visuals that unveiled it.

To reflect the grit of UVU's soccer players and the speed of the game, I used texture and motion throughout the identity.
