UVU License Plates Show Spirit and Support

When you get Utah Valley University license plates, not only are you showing your Wolverine spirit, but you’re also giving to scholarships that help current students.

This is an affordable and stylish way to give back to the university. A UVU license plate is $35 a year, and all proceeds go to scholarships.

Support UVU

Because the UVU option is a Utah custom license plate, it’s only available for vehicles registered in Utah.

It’s available to any Utah-based Wolverine fan, including community members, businesses, alumni, and students.

Order Your Plate Online (opens in a new tab)

Opens the Utah DMV Motor Vehicle Portal.

What Your Plate Will Look Like

The new design features the Wolverine head logo next to a new set of alphanumeric characters.

If you get one for a vehicle you already own, you keep your existing expiration and renewal dates. An annual tax-deductible $35 donation is included when you renew your registration.

Already Have One?

If you already have a Utah Valley University license plate, thank you! Your contribution has helped raise thousands of dollars for scholarships, benefiting many students.

How to Order Your UVU Plate

Online via DMV

  1. Select Change Your Plates and complete the validation steps.
  2. Select Special Group Plate.
  3. Choose whether to personalize your plate.
  4. For plate type, choose Utah Valley University.
  5. Continue through payment and submission.

In Person at the DMV

Request a special group plate for Utah Valley University. Bring a copy of one of these:

  • Vehicle registration
  • License plate number
  • Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)

At the Dealership

When purchasing a car, ask the dealership for a Utah Valley University plate. All Utah car dealerships can request UVU plates for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Is the Initial Cost of a License Plate?

You will need to pay the $21.50 one-time special group plate fee for standard vehicles or $20.50 for trailers (includes postage and handling) plus the $35 annual contribution to the scholarship fund. If you have already renewed your vehicle registration for the year, you won’t need to pay it again.

What if I Want to Personalize My UVU Plate?

You can order personalized plates with up to five alphanumeric characters.

Online via DMV: Select “Change Your Plates” then complete the validation steps. Under plate information for “I want to order a,” choose “Special Group Plate.” Select “Yes” to personalize your plate. For plate type, choose “Utah Valley University Prs.” Enter your desired characters and explanation, then continue through payment and submission.

At the DMV: Complete Form TC-817 and submit it with the personalized plate fee. On the form, type “Utah Valley University” under section 3, Plate and Character Selection, in the Utah Special Group Plate field.

Personalized plates cost a $66 personalized special group plate fee plus the $35 UVU scholarship donation at first. After that, a $10 personalized plate fee and the $35 donation are added to your vehicle registration each year. Learn more on the Utah DMV website (opens in a new tab).

Can I Use This as My UVU Parking Permit?

No. Students and employees still need to purchase a parking permit. Once you have one, you can park in specially marked parking spots.

Do I Need Front and Back UVU Plates?

No. Utah drivers are not required to have a front license plate as of Jan. 1, 2025.
