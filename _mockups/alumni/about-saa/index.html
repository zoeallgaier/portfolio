What Are Student Alumni Ambassadors?

Student Alumni Ambassadors (SAAs) are current UVU students who connect students across campus to the mission of the Alumni Relations office.

Our purpose is to create a “Forever Wolverines” attitude through events, programs, and information that enhance the student experience and prepare students to become engaged alumni after graduation.

Do I Qualify for the Scholarship?

If awarded, the scholarship provides up to $1,750 per semester (fall and spring) as funds allow. Recipients serve as a Student Alumni Ambassador from Aug. 15 to May 15.

To qualify, you must:

  1. Have a cumulative GPA of 3.0 or better
  2. Have at least 30 credit hours completed at UVU
  3. Maintain full-time student status (12+ credits per semester)
  4. Submit your application through Scholarship Universe with letters of recommendation
  5. Interview as a finalist, if selected

Applications open Nov. 1, 2025, and close Jan. 31, 2026, at 11:59 pm.

    Ambassador, 2024–25

    Read Savannah’s Story

    “I just finished up my year-long ambassador position at Utah Valley University as a Student Alumni Ambassador and absolutely loved it. I learned so much about UVU, how to be a more effective leader, how to record, edit, and post podcasts (check us out on all platforms- Forever Wolverines podcast), network with inspiring alumni and community members, and celebrate school spirit through service and homecoming activities. This role has been such a meaningful part of my college experience. From organizing events and engaging with alumni, to preserving UVU traditions and creating connections that stretch across generations of Wolverines—I’ve grown so much both personally and professionally.”
