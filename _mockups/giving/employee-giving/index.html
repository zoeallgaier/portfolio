More Than Colleagues

At UVU, we are more than just colleagues; we are a community united by a shared mission to shape the future through empowering students to achieve their educational dreams.

Your time, energy, and passion already make UVU a better place. Employee Giving is an ongoing invitation to amplify your support by giving back to programs and initiatives that matter most to you.

This is an opportunity to extend your passion further. Every gift, no matter the size, makes a difference in students’ lives now and in the future. This is your chance to make your impact go beyond what you already contribute every day.

I Am UVU

Employee Giving FAQ

Why Should I Give to UVU?

Your gift will support students and help fulfill UVU’s mission of providing exceptional care when you give to scholarship, program, or building funds.

It’s an easy way to show your support of UVU as an institution and as an employer.

Research shows that giving increases your overall happiness.

How Can I Donate?

The quickest and easiest method is via payroll deduction. You can set up or change your payroll deduction through the Employee Payroll Deduction Hub (opens in a new tab). You can also make a one-time or recurring gift on our donation page. If you prefer to give via check or cash, please contact Tess Fontanetti-Rice at tessf@uvu.edu.

What Is the Minimum Amount I Can Donate?

The minimum amount for payroll deduction is $1 per paycheck and $10 for credit card donations.

What Are the Advantages of Payroll Deduction?

Payroll deduction is fast and easy. A payroll deduction of just $5 per paycheck adds up to a $120 contribution by the end of the year. If every employee on campus gave just $5 per paycheck, it would equal nearly half a million dollars for the university to use for scholarships, program development, and building construction.

How Do I Set Up, Manage, or Change My Payroll Deduction?

Once you complete the Employee Payroll Deduction Form (opens in a new tab), the amount you specify will be automatically deducted from each paycheck. Please note that it may take one or two pay periods for a new or updated deduction to take effect due to the payroll processing cycle and the timing of form submission.

Your payroll deduction will continue until you choose to make a change. To manage your deduction, whether you want to adjust the amount, add a new gift, or cancel it altogether, revisit the Employee Giving Hub (opens in a new tab) and select the appropriate option:

  • Terminate my current donation
  • Replace it with a new donation
  • Keep my existing donation and add an additional one

A charitable receipt summarizing your payroll deductions will be mailed to you in January for tax purposes.

Can Part-Time Employees Donate Using Payroll Deduction?

Part-time employees may sign up for payroll deduction. If there is a pay period in which you did not receive a paycheck, no donations will be processed. Your usual donation amount will be deducted from your next paycheck. Donations will not be deducted “in arrears” in a cumulative amount.

What if I Don’t Want to Give Through Payroll Deduction?

One-time or recurring gifts are also appreciated and simple to do on our donation page. If you prefer to donate via check, stocks, property, using a donor-advised fund, or utilizing an IRA rollover, please contact Vicky Hopper at 801-863-5426 or vicky.hopper@uvu.edu.

Is My Gift Tax-Deductible?

Yes. All donations are processed through the Utah Valley University Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Please keep the official receipt for your tax records. Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Please consult with your tax advisor to determine if your gift is tax-deductible as a charitable contribution.

When Will I Receive My Receipt?

For one-time gifts, you will receive an email receipt right away. Please keep this for your records.

For payroll deductions, you will receive a receipt in January for all gifts made during the previous calendar year.

How Is Donating Different Than Giving to PACE?

PACE provides an opportunity to create a culture of exceptional care by contributing a non-charitable $0.50 per paycheck through the Goodwill Association. Funds are used to send well wishes and floral baskets for various reasons to UVU employees. Learn more about the Goodwill Association on myUVU.

Does My Donation Qualify for a Matching Gift?

Even though UVU does not offer a matching gift program, many employers will match a spouse’s charitable contribution and/or volunteer hours. To find out if a company you have connections with offers a matching gift program, see Matching Gifts. If your company is eligible, request a matching gift form and submit it to matchinggifts@uvu.edu.

What’s the Best Way to Leave a Legacy on Campus?

Gift Planning

Many people choose a long-term approach to giving using a bequest, will, annuity, or trust. The tax benefits vary depending on the type of gift chosen. Different gift types allow you access to your funds in case you need them, as your donation will take place after you have passed on. If you are interested, please visit us at uvgift.org (opens in a new tab).

Presidential Scholar Commemorative Bench

You may also leave a physical imprint on campus by naming a UVU bench. A named bench can also be used to honor the accomplishments of individuals or organizations or can be designated in memoriam of a loved one, professor, colleague, friend, etc. Your gift to the Presidential Commemorative Bench project will forever improve the campus landscape and help support the academic success of a UVU Presidential Scholar. Learn more about commemorative benches.
