Overview

A redesign of two key UVU ecosystems. For giving: a searchable directory and page for every college and initiative. For alumni: a top-down redesign. Perks, programs and more, made one tap away.

Creating a template system with Claude Code, I audited the UXUI of each page and designed 28 pages + PDF mockups in the span of a week; each one accessible, mobile-friendly, and CMS-ready.
