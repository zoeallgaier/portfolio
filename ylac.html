Overview

UVU's Young Living Alumni Center has four touch screens and a horizontal 4K screen. I designed each screen to be an extension of the system I built for giving & alumni pages: alumni benefits, giving, or donor info.

Left alone, each screen is animated and pulls its QR code to center every 25 seconds, so anyone across the room knows where to look.
