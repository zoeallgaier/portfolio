UVU Diplomas

Your School. Your Diploma.

Each UVU graduate is issued one free paper copy of their UVU diploma at the end of their graduating semester. Students who graduated after fall 2019 are also given one free digital copy.

  • Reissuing Diplomas

    Whether you graduated from CUVS, UTTI, UTC, UVCC, UVSC, or UVU with a certificate, associate, bachelor’s, or master’s degree, you are eligible to purchase a reissued UVU diploma ($25) to display in your home or office.

    Order a Reissued Diploma (opens in a new tab)

  • Check on a Recent Diploma

    If you have recently graduated and would like to check on the status of your diploma, contact the Graduation Office at 801-863-8438.

    Email the Graduation Office

  • Need Proof of Graduation?

    Please note that UVU considers UVU transcripts (not diplomas) as official documentation of graduation. If you need proof of graduation for a job or other purpose, you may want to ask if an official UVU transcript would suffice.

    Request an Official Transcript ($5)
