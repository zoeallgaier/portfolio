About Alumni Scholarships

The Alumni Association is pleased to offer scholarships to UVU students.

These scholarships are funded by the generous contributions of UVU alumni, students, employees, organizations, and community members.

Available Scholarships

Half Tuition, Fall and Spring

Future UVU Alumni Scholarship

For the child or ward of a UVU alum.

Eligibility

  • Child or ward of an alum who graduated from UVU, UVSC, UVCC, UTC at Provo, or UTTI
  • Sophomore at UVU with at least 30 credits (UVU or transfer)
  • Full-time student with a minimum 3.0 GPA
  • Utah resident
  • Seeking a bachelor’s degree at UVU

Your Application Must Include

  • The alum’s name (and the name used while attending, if it changed)
  • The alum’s current address, email, phone number, and UVID if known
  • A written statement describing any barriers to graduation you face

Application opens in November. Meeting the criteria doesn’t guarantee an award.

Up to $1,750 per Semester

Student Alumni Leadership Scholarship

For appointed members of the Student Alumni Ambassadors.

Eligibility

  • Appointed member of the Student Alumni Ambassadors
  • Strong leadership abilities
  • Cumulative GPA of 3.0 or better
  • At least one year of full-time attendance at UVU before receiving the scholarship
  • Full-time student (12+ credits)

Application Questions

  1. Describe the importance of giving to the next generation of UVU students, whether as a volunteer, advocate, donor, or source of advice and career support.
  2. Describe the qualities you possess that will make you an enthusiastic, dependable, and motivated UVU Student Alumni Ambassador.
  3. Share your volunteer and leadership experience and the lessons you learned from it.
  4. Describe in one sentence why you wish to be a UVU Student Alumni Ambassador.

Finalists will be invited to an interview. Awarded fall and spring semesters as funds allow.

Application opens in November. Meeting the criteria doesn’t guarantee an award.

How to Apply

The majority of UVU’s scholarship applications open Nov. 1 and close Jan. 31 at 11:59 pm.

Questions about eligibility or the application process? Contact UVU Financial Aid and Scholarships.
