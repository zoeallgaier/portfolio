UVU EverGREEN Campaign

Designer II  ·  $254M raised

Scope

EverGREEN was UVU's first campaign; which meant it had to feel serious to a $10M donor, and alive across 902+ projects. I designed the EverGREEN websites, events, proposals and newsletter.

Partnering with the UVU SMARTLab, I A/B tested designs, which provided the data to connect with our donors more deeply.

Results

EverGREEN raised $254,397,788.

Newsletter click-throughs went from 1.3% to 7.2%.

Giving-page engagement climbed to 96%.

Online giving rose 147%.
