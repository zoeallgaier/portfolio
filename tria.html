Tria

Social app  ·  Product design

Tria is a social media app built for real relationships. Whether you're keeping up with lifelong friends, or finding your people for the first time, Tria is the place to do it.

Your feed is chronological. There are no ads, and no algorithm deciding for you. Just a place to share your life, discover things worth caring about, and stay connected.

View Tria on the App Store →

I designed, coded, and shipped Tria myself; with Claude Code as my co-developer and Apple's Human Interface Guidelines as the standard I held it to.

The HIG makes you answer for real things: touch targets, Dynamic Type, safe areas, native navigation. Leveraging AI allowed me to focus on my strengths: the user experience, accessibility, and design.
