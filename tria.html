Tria

Social app  ·  Product design

Tria is a social media app I designed and built myself. After taking a two year hiatus from social media, I wanted to create a space that felt more authentic and less algorithmic.

On Tria, you can post like any other platform, and you can post activities/events. It's important to me that Tria fosters in-person connections as much as online ones.

Your feed is chronological and there are no ads. Just a place to share your life, discover things worth caring about, and stay connected.

View Tria on the App Store →

Tria has gained 84+ users since release, based on word of mouth sharing. Developing the app myself gave me complete control over the user experience and allowed me to implement features that prioritize authenticity and connection.
