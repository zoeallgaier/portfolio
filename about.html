A bit about me

I’m a multidisciplinary designer who’s spent eight years in the overlap between graphic, motion, and web. Not because I couldn’t pick one, but because pulling them apart is where brands lose the plot.

As Creative Director at Elysium Black Diamond, I created a brand that made $300K in eight months and is still going strong. As a Designer II at UVU, I lead design on fundraising campaigns and experiences.

Outside work, you can usually find me at the Natural History Museum, vibe-coding at a coffee shop, reading nonfiction, or out taking photos.
