I'm a multidisciplinary Designer who's spent eight years in the overlap between graphic, motion, and web. Not because I couldn't pick one, but because pulling them apart is where brands lose the plot.

My career started in videography, and evolved into digital design; so naturally, I'm a motion-first designer. I'm very lucky to have worked on a wide range of projects, from fundraising to app design.

As creative director at Elysium Black Diamond, I created a brand that made $300K in eight months. As a designer II at UVU, I lead design on campaigns that have raised over $254M. Same storytelling, bigger stakes.
