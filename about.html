A bit about me

I started my career eight years ago, at the beginning of the "make someone stop scrolling" era. Obsessed with telling stories through video, I focused on narrative, advertising, and social media strategy.

But videography can only get you so far. So I spent 4 years learning the ins and outs of graphic design. Print, digital, branding, logo animations, the works.

As creative director at Elysium Black Diamond, I built a brand from zero and watched it clear $300K in eight months. As a designer II at UVU, I lead design on campaigns that have raised over $254M. Same storytelling, bigger stakes. 👩🏻‍💻
