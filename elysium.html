Elysium Black Diamond

Creative Director  ·  $300K revenue

Elysium had a great product but no brand, site, content, or shop. As Creative Director, I built an identity, Shopify, email, social, packaging, product photography, then launched it into the market as one piece.

To move fast without losing the plot, I built an asset library of product shots, ad templates, and brand guidelines. Enough runway that one person could run like a full design team. Then I documented everything so the brand could keep running after I handed it off.

ElysiumBlack.com cleared $300K in it's first eight months. ElysiumHope.com hit $10K+ monthly. Combined social grew to 80K+ followers on the back of 500+ assets I designed, shot, and shipped myself.
