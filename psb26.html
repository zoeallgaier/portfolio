President's Scholarship Ball 2026

Creative Lead  ·  October 10, 2026

UVU's President's Scholarship Ball is the largest donor event of the year. For 2026 I'm leading out on the main design: the concept, the system it becomes, and how it carries across the night.

The direction is Future in Focus: one key message stretched across print, motion, digital signage, and the event space, so donors have a relationship with the event before even attending.

The ball is October 10, 2026. Full case study to follow.

Future in Focus brings clarity to UVU's existing pillars: exceptional care, accountability, and results. The design system is built around singular focal points, with a hex grid that nods to the architecture on campus.

Coming into a presidential transition, the concept is deliberately about the path forward: clear, steady, and built to earn donor trust.
