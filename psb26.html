President's Scholarship Ball 2026

Design Lead  ·  $792.3K raised

Overview

President's Scholarship Ball is UVU's largest donor event of the year. For 2026 I created and led the design: Future in Focus.

Coming into a presidential transition, Future in Focus was about the path forward: clear, steady, and built to earn donor trust.

The system is built around singular focal points, with a hex grid that nods to the campus community and architecture.

Results

  • $792.3Kraised on the night
  • 460total guests from the UVU donor community
  • 120event assets I designed, oversaw, and approved
