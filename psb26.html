President's Scholarship Ball 2026

Creative Lead  ·  October 10, 2026

Overview

President's Scholarship Ball is UVU's largest donor event of the year. For 2026 I'm leading the main design: the concept, the system it becomes, and how it carries across the night.

Coming into a presidential transition, the concept is about the path forward: clear, steady, and built to earn donor trust.

Concept

Future in Focus brings clarity to UVU's existing pillars: exceptional care, accountability, and results. The design system is built around singular focal points, with a hex grid that nods to the architecture on campus.

The ball is October 10, 2026. Full case study to follow.
