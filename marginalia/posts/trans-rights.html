My Response to Trump’s Attempts to Remove Trans Rights

post – February 17, 2025

Before gender-affirming care, my body and I were strangers. Since puberty there has been a strong disconnect between my mind/soul and my physical body. It’s a grotesque and alienating feeling that I wouldn’t wish on anyone.

I looked in the mirror and saw someone I did not know. Sometimes still do. I was unable to participate in my own life, only watch as someone else’s life passed in front of me. This story is not uncommon within the trans community.

Eventually, I reached my breaking point. I realized I could no longer see a future for myself. I had self-medicated by trying to embrace masculinity, but it was facade. A performance. I became numb, tired, and broken down. Running on fumes.

So, I made a fundamental change – I sought gender-affirming care, to align my body with my spirit.

Immediately after beginning treatment I noticed my thoughts and emotions became clearer. As time went on my relationships deepened. I learned to smile, and I began to finally see myself in the mirror. Being trans is not a choice, but choosing to seek medical help made my life livable for the first time since puberty.

The U.S. Office of Personnel Management’s announcement that it will exclude coverage for gender-affirming care for adults from the Federal Employee Health Benefits (FEHB) and Postal Service Health Benefits (PSHB) programs beginning in 2026 is devastating.

The wave of bills and executive orders are eerily similar to the legislation enacted in Germany in the 1940s. The current administration is drenched in a hatred for diverse peoples and experiences; something I believe will have dire consequences for future generations.

This as part of a well-funded campaign against transgender people by the Trump administration and its financiers. Not only is it a clear violation of civil and human rights, but it is soleley for profits sake, which is deeply disturbing.

Denying healthcare that saves lives is evil. Full stop.
